Did You Know?

Layer Poultry Facts You Should Know

Understanding layer farming helps you appreciate the quality in every egg you eat.

🥚

📅 Egg Production Rate

A well-managed layer hen produces 250–300 eggs per year — nearly one egg every single day when properly fed and cared for.

💪

🍳 Nutritional Powerhouse

One egg contains 6–7g of high-quality protein, 13 essential vitamins and minerals — one of nature's most complete foods.

🌾

🍽️ Feed & Egg Quality

The hen's diet directly affects egg quality. Our hens eat layers mash rich in calcium, protein, and vitamins — giving you deep yellow yolks and strong shells.

💉

🛡️ Vaccination is Key

Layer hens must be vaccinated against Newcastle Disease, Gumboro, and Fowl Pox to ensure every egg is safe to eat.

🌡️

🏠 Housing & Stress

Proper housing with good ventilation and adequate space keeps hens stress-free. Stressed hens lay fewer eggs with thinner shells.

🥚

📦 How to Store Eggs

Store eggs in a cool, dry place or refrigerator. Properly stored eggs stay fresh up to 3–5 weeks. Never wash eggs before storage.
